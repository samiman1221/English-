Grammar ✎

Learn English grammar step by step.

Nouns

A noun is a word used to name a person, place, animal, thing, or idea.

Examples: teacher, school, book, happiness

Pronouns

A pronoun is used instead of a noun.

Examples: I, you, he, she, it, we, they

Verbs

A verb describes an action or state.

Examples: run, eat, study, write

Adjectives

An adjective describes a noun or pronoun.

Examples: beautiful, big, intelligent, happy

Adverbs

An adverb describes how, when, or where an action happens.

Examples: quickly, slowly, carefully

Prepositions

A preposition shows a relationship between words.

Examples: in, on, at, under, between

12 English Tenses ⏰

Master all twelve English tenses.

01 · Simple Present

Used for habits and general facts.

S + V1

I study English every day.

02 · Present Continuous

Used for actions happening now.

S + am/is/are + V-ing

I am studying English.

03 · Present Perfect

Used for completed actions connected to now.

S + have/has + V3

I have studied English.

04 · Present Perfect Continuous

Action continuing up to now.

S + have/has been + V-ing

I have been studying.

05 · Simple Past

Completed action in the past.

S + V2

I studied yesterday.

06 · Past Continuous

Action happening at a past time.

S + was/were + V-ing

I was studying.

07 · Past Perfect

Action completed before another past action.

S + had + V3

I had studied before the test.

08 · Past Perfect Continuous

Continuing action before another past event.

S + had been + V-ing

I had been studying.

09 · Simple Future

Used for future actions.

S + will + V1

I will study tomorrow.

10 · Future Continuous

Action continuing at a future time.

S + will be + V-ing

I will be studying.

11 · Future Perfect

Action completed before a future time.

S + will have + V3

I will have studied.

12 · Future Perfect Continuous

Action continuing up to a future time.

S + will have been + V-ing

I will have been studying.
