📝 Grammar

Learn the basic rules of English grammar.

1. Nouns

A noun is a person, place, animal, or thing.

Examples: teacher, school, book, dog

2. Pronouns

Pronouns replace nouns.

Examples: I, you, he, she, it, we, they

3. Verbs

A verb describes an action or state.

Examples: run, eat, read, write, sleep

4. Adjectives

An adjective describes a noun.

Examples: beautiful, big, small, happy

5. Adverbs

An adverb describes how an action happens.

Examples: quickly, slowly, carefully

⏰ 12 English Tenses

1. Simple Present

2. Present Continuous

3. Present Perfect

Example: I have studied English.

Example: I have been studying.
